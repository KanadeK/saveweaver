# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

Be respectful, assume good intent while discussing evidence, accept
constructive feedback, and focus on the impact of behavior. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the
maintainer contact options on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed with
care and confidentiality.
